# ContextCue problem and scope

Hypothesis: international students entering Singapore may understand individual English words but struggle to interpret unfamiliar communication cues and choose a response. This is not yet validated by interviews.

Audience: international students of any nationality in Singapore. English explanations by default. The user supplies an actual situation; campus teamwork, humour and feedback are the initial scenarios.

Value: understand possible meanings and uncertainty, clarify rather than assume, practise a respectful reply. Singapore is the initial data scope, not a claim that every ambiguous interaction is uniquely Singaporean.

Peer-sourced local context (#6) is the intended knowledge foundation. The current dataset is synthetic and awaiting peers; context-aware learning (#2), humour (#7), and feedback (#8) are integrated scenarios.

Success for the implemented prototype: situation → relevant evidence or explicit no-match → explanation/reference distinction → practice and feedback/self-check. Real learning outcomes require student testing.
